# Design notes

Visual lock: the Attio-inspired landing screenshot (lilac bloom, screenshot product card, Inter). Do not restyle into another product’s palette or type.

**Motion:** follow [`design/animations-rules.md`](./animations-rules.md) for all frontend work. Patterns A/B/C, easing `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Motion only — do not adopt a reference site’s colors, serif, or button radius.
